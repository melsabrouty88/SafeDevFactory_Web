SafeTabCraft

Instantly tame browser chaos. One-click duplicate removal, automatic domain grouping, and lightning-fast tab search. Zero bloat.

Download for Chrome

Core Features

🗑️ Close Duplicates

Automatically detects and closes tabs with the same URL, freeing up RAM instantly and keeping your workspace clean.

📑 Group Tabs

Neatly groups your open tabs by their website (hostname), making it incredibly easy to navigate between related pages.

✨ Clean & Group

Do both at once! A single click closes all duplicate tabs and groups the remaining ones logically using Chrome's native Tab Groups.

🔍 Quick Tab Search

A live search bar in the popup to filter and instantly jump to any open tab across all windows. (Pro Feature)

🪟 Cross-Window Support

Extend tab cleaning and grouping across all open browser instances, either per-window or by consolidating them. (Pro Feature)

⚡ Keyboard Shortcuts

Trigger cleanup, grouping, and search without ever clicking the mouse. Power-user ready. (Pro Feature)
